Privacy Policy

Plain-language privacy for Andersons Plumbing Co.

What we collect

When you call, message us on WhatsApp, or send the quote form, we collect only what we need to answer you: your name, phone number, email address (if you give one), your suburb, the problem you describe, and any photo you choose to send.

How we use it

We use your details only to contact you about your request, arrange a visit, and give you a quote after diagnosis. We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to any marketing list.

How long we keep it

We keep request details only as long as needed to handle your job and any follow-up. Ask us to delete your details and we will.

Third parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you message us on WhatsApp or Facebook, those services handle your message under their own privacy policies.
