What to do first

Before we arrive

Four steps that limit the damage while help is on the way.

1

Turn off the water

Find your main stopcock and turn it off.

2

Turn off the electricity

If water is near plugs or your geyser, switch off at the DB board.

3

Move valuables

Lift rugs, electronics and furniture out of the water.

4

Call us
